Stop your own pickaxe, your own explosives and a stray attack from taking your base apart.

Place a No Break Pylon, press E, and everything inside the square around it becomes
indestructible — chests, walls, floors, machines, the pylon itself. Press E again and your base is
ordinary again, so you can remodel it. Protection is never global and never permanent: it is a
thing you switch on, in a place you chose.

WHAT YOU GET

  No Break Pylon     Place it, press E. The on/off state survives saving and loading.
  Pylon Workbench    Where the pylon, lens and remote are made. Crafted at an Automation Table.
  Pylon Lens         Hold it to see the edge of every switched-on pylon's square.
                     Nothing else ever draws the outline.
  Pylon Remote       Switch a pylon from across the room, for when you have walled
                     yourself out of reach of one.

WHILE A PYLON IS ON

Nothing inside its square can be broken, walls and ore included. That is the point rather than an
oversight: a base you cannot blow up by accident is also a base you cannot remodel by accident, so
switch the pylon off when you want to dig.

Farming and drill automation are the exception and keep working with it on. Anything that pays out
every time it is damaged, a drill's ore boulder above all, is never protected — protecting one
would duplicate resources and break a save permanently. That rule outranks everything else here.

MULTIPLAYER

The server decides what is protected and what a switch does, so every player sees the same thing.
There is no ownership: a pylon protects whatever is inside it, whoever placed it.

SETTINGS

  protectionDiameter  21     width and height, in tiles, of the square one pylon covers
  blockMobDamage      true   off leaves mobs and explosions able to destroy protected things
  showRangeWithLens   true   draw the outline while the lens is held
  remoteReachTiles    30     how far the remote reaches
